# Unfinished and known limitations

- **Sample data only.** The 4 plans, their insurers, prices and contact details are fictional and labelled "Sample plan". Real product data and real insurer or agent contacts still need to come from the organisers.
- **Comparison is limited to 2 plans at a time.**
- **English only.** Luganda, Swahili and other language versions are not built.
- **No price filter or sorting.** Premiums are charged over different periods (monthly or quarterly), and we chose not to convert between them automatically.
- **Checklist ticks are not saved.** They reset when the page reloads. This is deliberate, so that no user data is stored.
- **No "last updated" date** on plans yet. Add one when real data arrives.
- **Accessibility:** the term popup closes on Escape and on a tap outside it, but focus is not fully trapped inside it. No screen-reader audit has been done.
- **Tested at a 390px phone viewport in Chrome emulation only.** Not yet tested on real low-end Android phones or slow networks.
